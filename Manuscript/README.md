# Earlier draft

`Manuscript.md` and `Manuscript.docx` are a draft from March 2026. They do not match the paper accepted by *Genomics & Informatics*.

In particular, that draft treats the stored heterozygosity ratio as comparable across the sexes and reports a single confirmatory conclusion for the classifiers. The accepted paper corrects both points. The analyses and figures that match the accepted paper are in `../Revision/`. The repository `README.md` states the study as published.
